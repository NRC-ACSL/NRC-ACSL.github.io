Today we are releasing the first public report from the **CyberGym-CA** project. *(Placeholder text.)*

## Key findings

1. Current agents detect most single-stage attacks but struggle with slow, multi-stage intrusions.
2. Agents with explicit planning and memory contained threats faster.
3. Safety guardrails reduced harmful actions with little cost to performance.

## What's next

We will open the benchmark to community submissions early next year.
