Who we are

Mission & vision

Mission

To advance the science of autonomous cyber systems and give Canada and its partners trusted, open tools to defend digital infrastructure.

Vision

A world where autonomous cyber defence is safe, transparent and accountable, and where it strengthens human operators rather than replacing them.

Rigour

Reproducible methods, open benchmarks, and peer-reviewed results.

Openness

We release code, data and findings so others can build on them.

Responsibility

Human oversight and safety are built into every system we design.

Collaboration

We work with academia, industry and government, in Canada and internationally.
